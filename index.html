Zyntrix Auto poster

Developer: Zyntrix Studio  |  Last updated: August 13, 2026

Zyntrix Auto poster is an automated content publishing application that generates short, technology-related text posts and publishes them to the Facebook Page The Zyntrix Studio using the Meta Graph API.

About the app

  • Generates posts using AI text-generation services (Groq / Google Gemini).
  • Publishes text-only posts directly to the owner's Facebook Page.
  • Does not interact with personal profiles and does not post on behalf of any other person or Page.
  • Does not collect or store personal information of end users.
