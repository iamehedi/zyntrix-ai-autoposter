Data Deletion Request

App: Zyntrix Auto poster  |  Owner: Zyntrix Studio  |  Last updated: August 13, 2026

The Zyntrix Auto poster app is an automated publishing tool that generates and posts text content to the Facebook Page The Zyntrix Studio on behalf of its owner. It does not have user accounts and does not collect personal information from end users.

What data does the app store?

  • Facebook Page Access Token and Page ID — used only to publish posts to the owner's Page. Stored encrypted in GitHub Secrets, never committed to the repository, and deleted when the owner revokes or replaces the token.
  • Post history — topics, post IDs, and timestamps of published posts, stored in the app's repository so topics are not repeated.

The app does not store any personal information about Facebook users, Page followers, or visitors.

How to request data deletion

If you believe the app holds data related to you and you want it deleted, send a deletion request to the app owner:

  • Contact Zyntrix Studio via the Facebook Page The Zyntrix Studio, or
  • Open an issue on the app's GitHub repository: github.com/iamehedi/zyntrix-ai-autoposter/issues

In your request, state clearly that you are requesting deletion of data held by the Zyntrix Auto poster app. The owner will respond within 30 days.

What happens after you request deletion

Upon verification of the request, the app owner will delete the relevant stored data (e.g., remove post history entries or revoke tokens) and confirm the deletion to you.
